Introduction to Blood Donation

About Blood Donation

1. What is Blood Donation?

Blood donation is the process of voluntarily giving blood to help patients who need blood during medical treatment, surgery or emergencies.

2. Importance of Blood Donation

Blood donation helps save lives. Donated blood is used in hospitals for patients who need transfusions.

3. Benefits of Blood Donation

Blood donation supports community healthcare and helps maintain an adequate blood supply for patients.

4. Why is Blood Donation Needed?

Blood is needed for accident victims, surgeries, cancer treatments and people with certain blood disorders.

Uses of Donated Blood

Where is Donated Blood Used?

1. Accident Patients

Accident victims may lose a large amount of blood. Blood transfusions can help replace the lost blood and support their medical treatment.

2. Surgery Patients

Some patients need blood during or after major surgeries to replace blood lost during the operation.

3. Cancer Patients

Some cancer patients need blood or platelet transfusions during their treatment.

4. Pregnant Women

Women who experience serious blood loss during childbirth may require a blood transfusion.

5. Blood Disorder Patients

Patients with certain blood disorders, such as thalassemia, may need regular blood transfusions as part of their treatment.

Who Can Donate Blood?

Basic Eligibility Requirements

  • Generally, the donor must be between 18 and 65 years old.
  • The donor must meet the minimum weight requirement for the donation type.
  • The donor should be in good general health.
  • Haemoglobin level must meet the required medical standard.
  • The donor must pass the medical screening at the blood centre.

Who Should Wait Before Donating?

  • People who currently have fever or an active infection.
  • People who are feeling unwell.
  • People who are pregnant or have recently given birth.
  • People who have recently undergone surgery or certain medical treatments.
  • People advised to postpone donation by a healthcare professional.

Before Blood Donation

  • Eat a healthy meal.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Get adequate rest.
  • Carry an accepted identity document.
  • Answer all medical screening questions honestly.
Important Medical Note: These are general educational guidelines. Final eligibility depends on medical screening by authorised blood-centre staff. People under 18 must not donate blood.
